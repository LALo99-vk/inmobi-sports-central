Update the Matches / Tournament Bracket UI based on these requirements.

The current bracket is too plain. The connecting arrows/lines between rounds are very important and should be a major visual part of the bracket.

## Bracket

Redesign the bracket so that:

- Clear connecting lines/arrows visually connect each match to the next round.
- The progression from Round 1 → Round 2 → Quarter-Finals → Semi-Finals → Final is immediately understandable.
- The connectors should be visually prominent, polished, and intentional.
- Do not use simple thin lines that make the bracket look basic.
- Each match should align naturally with the corresponding match in the next round.
- The bracket should clearly communicate how the winner of one match moves into the next round.
- The bracket should feel like a professional sports tournament bracket.
- Keep the overall design clean, classy, exciting, and easy to follow.
- Do not clutter the screen with unnecessary elements.

## Match Participants

The participant information shown in the bracket depends on the type of sport.

### Individual / Doubles Sports

For sports such as:

- Carrom
- Badminton
- Table Tennis

Show the actual participant names.

Support both:

- Singles — one player
- Doubles — two players

Examples:

PLAYER 1
PLAYER 2

or:

PLAYER 1 & PLAYER 2
PLAYER 3 & PLAYER 4

### Team Sports

For team-based sports such as:

- Cricket
- Football

Show ONLY the team name in the bracket.

Do NOT show the individual players inside the bracket match cards.

For example:

TEAM A        2
TEAM B        1

Player details will be handled later in a separate Match Details view, so do not add that functionality now.

## Match States

Continue supporting:

- Upcoming
- Live
- Completed
- Winner

Completed matches should clearly indicate the winner and score.

Live matches should have a subtle but noticeable LIVE indicator.

Upcoming matches should clearly show the scheduled time.

## Design

Keep the existing InMobi visual style and overall website structure.

Do not add new sections or unnecessary features.

The main focus should now be:

1. Beautiful and professional knockout bracket
2. Clear and visually strong arrows/connectors between rounds
3. Actual participant names for individual/doubles sports
4. Team names only for team sports
5. Clear scores and match states
6. Clean, classy, exciting, and smooth user experience
